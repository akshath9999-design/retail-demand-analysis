Model Selection & Architecture Rationale

1. Chronological Split (No Data Leakage)

Retail sales exhibit strong temporal dependencies. Rather than random k-fold cross-validation, models were trained strictly on observations prior to November 1, 2011, and tested on the subsequent unseen holiday period (Nov 1 – Dec 9, 2011).

2. Feature Engineering Pipeline

Constructed strictly past-looking lag features (lag 1, 7, 14, 30 days), rolling averages (7, 14, 30 days), demand volatility (7-day standard deviation), momentum trend ratios, and calendar indicators (day of week, weekend, month).

3. Gradient Boosting Superiority

Gradient Boosting outperformed Linear Regression and Random Forest with the lowest RMSE (71.81) and highest R² (0.325) because decision trees effectively capture non-linear step changes while sequential boosting corrects residual errors from high-demand surge events.
